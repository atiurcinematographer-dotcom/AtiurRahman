About

Mohammad Atiur Rahman

Documentary Filmmaker

I'm an independent Bangladeshi documentary filmmaker, editor, cinematographer and visual storyteller.

My work focuses on documenting real people, traditions, culture, heritage and overlooked places of Bangladesh through cinematic visual storytelling.

I want to create meaningful films that inspire, preserve memories and encourage positive social change.
